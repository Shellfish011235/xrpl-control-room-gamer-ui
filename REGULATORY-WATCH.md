## Why This Matters

- **US:** President/White House meetings and executive orders can shift federal posture on crypto and AI. State laws (e.g. Colorado AI Act) add compliance obligations.
- **EU:** EU AI Act full enforcement (Aug 2026); high-risk fintech AI needs risk assessments, transparency, and audit trails.
- **XRPL/crypto:** GENIUS Act (payment stablecoins/AML), CLARITY Act discussions, SEC/CFTC boundaries—all affect how we position the product (sim vs testnet vs mainnet, custody vs non-custody).

## Where to Check (Stay Current)

### US Federal / White House

- **White House / President:** [whitehouse.gov](https://www.whitehouse.gov) — press releases, briefings, executive orders (e.g. digital assets, AI).
- **SEC:** [sec.gov/news](https://www.sec.gov/news) — enforcement, policy, digital asset guidance.
- **CFTC:** [cftc.gov/PressRoom](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/index.htm) — commodity/digital asset developments.
- **FinCEN:** [fincen.gov/news](https://www.fincen.gov/news) — BSA/AML, money transmission.

### US State

- **Colorado AI Act:** Effective June 2026 — high-risk AI systems; risk assessments and oversight. Search: “Colorado AI Act 2026.”
- **Florida:** OFR, Ch. 560 (money transmission), digital asset/UCC updates. [flofr.com](https://www.flofr.com) or Florida Legislature site.

### EU / International

- **EU AI Act:** [eur-lex.europa.eu](https://eur-lex.europa.eu) — search “AI Act”; full enforcement Aug 2026.
- **MiCA (crypto):** EU regulation for crypto assets; check EU official sources and summaries (e.g. [europa.eu](https://europa.eu)).

### Crypto / XRPL Ecosystem

- **Ripple:** [ripple.com](https://ripple.com), [ripple.com/insights](https://ripple.com/insights) — policy, custody, WaaS, regulatory commentary.
- **XRPL Foundation:** [xrpl.org](https://xrpl.org), [foundation.xrpl.org](https://foundation.xrpl.org).
- **Legal/crypto summaries:** [bakerdonelson.com](https://www.bakerdonelson.com), [globallegalinsights.com](https://www.globallegalinsights.com) — often have “crypto regulation 2026” or “AI Act” summaries.

### AI in Finance (High-Risk / Explainability)

- **EU AI Act (high-risk):** Transparency, human oversight, audit trails, incident reporting. [nortal.com](https://www.nortal.com), [zodia-custody.com](https://zodia-custody.com) for agentic AI / provenance.
- **US:** Track state AI laws (Colorado, etc.) and any federal AI framework (NIST, executive orders).

## Staying Compliant: Process

To keep the project **aligned with current regulations** and compliant as we build:

1. **Re-check sources on a schedule**  
   Periodically (e.g. quarterly, or after any major regulatory news) open the links under “Where to Check” above and scan for new guidance, enforcement, or law changes that affect crypto, digital assets, or AI in finance.

2. **Update the compliance doc when things change**  
   When laws, dates, or interpretations change (e.g. EU AI Act enforcement date, new state AI law, SEC/CFTC guidance):  
   - Update [COMPLIANCE-GLOBAL-US-FLORIDA.md](./COMPLIANCE-GLOBAL-US-FLORIDA.md) with the new detail or date.  
   - If a new jurisdiction or topic becomes relevant, add a short subsection or bullet there and a pointer in this file.

3. **When we add or change product features**  
   - If the feature touches **payments, custody, trading, or AI suggestions**: add or update the relevant section in COMPLIANCE-GLOBAL-US-FLORIDA.md (e.g. “Phase 1 Paper Trading Features” or a new feature table).  
   - Re-check the regulatory sources above for any new rules that might apply (e.g. new state AI law, updated money-transmission guidance).  
   - Before **real users or mainnet**: reassess with qualified counsel; do not rely on this doc alone.

4. **Feature checklist (stay current)**  
   - [ ] New feature documented in COMPLIANCE-GLOBAL-US-FLORIDA.md if it touches money, custody, or AI.  
   - [ ] REGULATORY-WATCH sources checked for recent updates.  
   - [ ] ROADMAP.md and phase (sim / testnet / mainnet) still accurate.  
   - [ ] Before mainnet or real-user flows: legal advice obtained and documented.

This process is manual; a human (or scheduled review) should run it. Regulations change; staying compliant means repeating these steps as the product and the law evolve.
